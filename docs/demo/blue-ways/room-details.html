Ocean Villa

Home / Rooms & Villas / Ocean Villa

Our signature villa on stilts: a private deck over the lagoon, a glass floor panel above the reef and a butler on call.

The villa

Ninety square metres of timber and linen, reached by a lantern-lit jetty. Wake to the lagoon under the glass floor panel, swim from your own steps and eat breakfast on the deck in your robe.

Inside there is a king bed facing the water, a daybed for afternoon naps, a deep tub by the window and an outdoor rain shower. A butler looks after dinner bookings, spa times and the minibar, which is restocked daily with local juices and beers.

Good to know

Check-in from 2 pm, check-out by 12 noon. Villa guests can keep the villa until 4 pm when the next night is free — ask the butler the day before.

The deck has a rail and a gate on the lagoon steps. Children under twelve swim with an adult; life vests in every size are in the villa.

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. After that, the first night is charged.
