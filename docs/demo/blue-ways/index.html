Welcome

About Blue-ways

Blue-ways sits where the palms meet the lagoon: forty-eight rooms and villas, two pools, a spa and a kitchen that cooks with the morning's catch.

We have been welcoming guests since 1998, and most of our team has been here almost as long. Expect a room that is ready early, a table that remembers how you like your coffee, and a concierge who knows which beach is quiet at four o'clock.

Come See Our Brand New Ballroom And Spa

The ballroom seats two hundred beneath a hand-painted ceiling, with its own terrace for sunset cocktails — made for weddings, galas and the kind of birthday people talk about.

Downstairs, the spa has six treatment rooms, a hammam and a salt-water plunge pool. Treatments use island coconut, lemongrass and sea salt, and every stay includes one free visit to the thermal suite.
