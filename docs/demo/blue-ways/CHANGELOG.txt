Blueways — Responsive HTML5 Hotel Reservation Website Template
==============================================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
-------------------

- A completely new design, "Chart Room": a nautical-logbook look with
  Libre Caslon headlines, IBM Plex text and coordinates, chart paper,
  ink navy and lagoon blue, depth-contour lines, photos framed as chart
  plates, square buttons and a chart-legend footer with a compass.
  Every page, header and footer was redesigned; all of the original
  pages, links and headings remain.
- A new header: a left navigation column on desktop with a Book a stay
  button, and a plain top bar with a full menu on tablets and phones.
- Rebuilt on Bootstrap 5 with every behaviour in plain JavaScript
  (js/main.js): the three sliders, the gallery lightbox, smooth scrolling
  and the section highlight in the menu, back-to-top, tabs, accordions
  and every form. Font Awesome 4 -> 6; all fonts are self-hosted.
- Four new pages: rooms & villas (six stays with instant filters), room
  details (photo switcher, particulars, policies and a live price
  quote), reservations (dates, room, extras, guest details, a running
  total and a confirmation with a reference) and the restaurant (hours,
  a tabbed menu and a table-reservation form).
- Three ready-to-send HTML emails, one per form: contact auto-reply,
  booking confirmation and table reservation (email-*.html). They adapt
  to phones.
- Home page: a check-availability bar under the hero that carries its
  dates into the reservations page, room slides with particulars and
  prices, amenities, packages, a ballroom & spa section, a gallery of
  twelve photos, testimonials, a weekly deal with a live countdown and
  a working contact form.
- Real hotel copy replaces the placeholder text on every page, and the
  element pages (accordions, alerts, buttons, form fields, grid, icons,
  messages, tabs, typography) are restyled to match.
- Works from phone to desktop: two columns on tablets, one photo per
  row in the phone gallery, no sideways scrolling from 320px.
